FAQ'S

Frequently Asked Questions

What services do you offer?

We offer haircuts, styling, coloring, treatments, makeup, facials, and more. Some branches also offer bridal packages and nail services.

Do I need to book an appointment in advance?

While walk-ins are welcome, we recommend booking in advance to ensure availability with your preferred stylist or time slot.

What hair products do you use?

We use high-quality, salon-grade products from trusted brands to protect and enhance your hair’s health and shine.

Do you offer services for men and children?

Yes! We provide haircuts and grooming services for men and kids in a comfortable, family-friendly environment.

Do you offer bridal and party makeup?

Yes, we specialize in bridal, party, and event makeup with customized packages for individuals and groups.

Do you offer any loyalty programs?

Yes! We offer seasonal discounts, referral bonuses, and loyalty cards for regular clients. Ask at the front desk or follow us online for updates.

Are your stylists trained and certified?

Yes. All of our hairstylists are trained professionals with certifications and ongoing education in the latest techniques and trends.
